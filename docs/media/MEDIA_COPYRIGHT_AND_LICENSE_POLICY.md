# Media Copyright And License Policy

Status: scaffolded in MEDIA-03.

This policy records operational license and copyright risk checks for creative media. It is not legal advice.

## License Review Required

License review is required before generation when:

- A provider/model license is unknown.
- Commercial use is requested.
- A prompt references copyrighted characters, franchises, brands, protected artwork, or uncertain style/identity claims.
- A provider is paid/cloud-based or has quota/terms constraints.
- Output will be used in client, product, advertising, social, or monetized contexts.

## Provider Metadata

The MEDIA-03 license report is metadata-only:

- `mock` is `test_only` and grants no production usage rights.
- `comfyui` is planned and requires model/provider license review.
- `external_paid` is blocked until explicit terms and paid-use policy are reviewed.

## Required Future Evidence

Before a provider can be user-ready, records must link to:

- provider terms
- model card or license
- commercial-use status
- attribution requirements
- restrictions on likeness, brands, public figures, adult content, political content, and medical/legal/financial claims
- output retention and asset metadata policy

No command may claim commercial rights without evidence.

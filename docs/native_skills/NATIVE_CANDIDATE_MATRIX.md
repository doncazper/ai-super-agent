# Native Candidate Matrix

Date: 2026-05-23

Scoring scale: 1 is low, 5 is high. For safety, implementation difficulty, dependency risk, and personal-data risk, higher scores mean more concern or stronger control needs. Workspace-bounded feasibility and testability higher scores are better.

All implementation paths are local-native translations. External skill text is not trusted and must not be installed or executed during intake.

## Matrix Notes

- `now` means safe to specify next, not necessarily safe to implement without a separate prompt.
- `soon` means likely valuable after the native vetter and manifest exist.
- `later` means defer until personal-data or side-effect gates are stronger or live-validated.
- `defer` means do not build until a separate decision record and explicit approval gate are complete.
